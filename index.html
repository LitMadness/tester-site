Wit, Warmth, and Humor Bleeding onto the Page

Larissa's Historical Regencies are a little literary, a lot of fun, and full of life, love, and heartwarming emotion—all wrapped in laugh-out-loud moments and the richly authentic language of the era.

Every word choice is researched. Every story steeped in Regency cant and period prose, woven in with enough wit and warmth that you'll find yourself fluent in the language of 1815 before you've turned ten pages. ;-)

If you've ever wished Jane Austen's novels had a little more heat, or you've been chasing that spark you found in the Signet Regencies of Mary Balogh, Carla Kelly, and Barbara Metzger,  you might've just found your next favorite author.

Step inside. Turn the page. Escape to another time.
