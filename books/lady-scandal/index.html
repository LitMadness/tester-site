Lady Scandal

Unheard of!She posted a public advertisement for a husband.

But only one man will learn her true identity…

Juliet, Lady Letheridge, cares naught if she scandalizes the ton by advertising anonymously for a husband. The recent young widow has already endured one miserable marriage, and this time she’s determined to decide her own fate.

Zeus Tanner has no inkling who the mysterious Lady Scandal is, and he could not care less. It’s her lands he covets at any cost, even if it means sacrificing his freedom.

Neither anticipate the sizzling encounter that follows. Despite Juliet shielding her identity during their interview and Zeus not being forthcoming about his true intentions, sparks fly.

But when their mutual attraction proves far greater than mere sparks, and Zeus learns the estate he craves may no longer exist, he is faced with a wrenching dilemma…

Abandon his bid to win her hand in marriage? Or throw caution, and stockings, to the wind, and allow them both to follow their hearts?

Lady Scandal is the first book in a fun, adult Regency romance series perfect for readers who love witty banter, humor, and steamy scandals resulting in feel-good happily ever afters.
