Larissa Lyons

Larissa's Contemporary Romance

When Larissa isn't escaping to Regency England, she's writing under the name Macy West — and the heroines are just as sassy, though the heroes have traded their cravats for beards.

Macy's spicy, feel-good romances feature big, burly alpha men — mountain men, ex-military protectors, bikers and cowboys — who fall hard and fast for the strong women destined to bring them to their knees. Always fun, always heartwarming, always a guaranteed HEA with no cheating and no cliffhangers.

If that sounds like your kind of escape, come find Macy at MacyWestauthor.com.
